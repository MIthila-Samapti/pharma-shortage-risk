WHAT THIS IS

Among drugs already on the FDA's shortage list, an estimate of whether the listing will end in a permanent discontinuation, versus resolve as a temporary gap.

HOW IT WORKS

A gradient-boosted model trained on public openFDA shortage data, validated with combination-grouped cross-validation (~0.93 AUC) so the score reflects generalization to unseen drug profiles, not memorized combinations. Supplier concentration — how many other manufacturers still list the drug — is the strongest signal, alongside dosage form and therapeutic category. A separate survival analysis cross-checks the same driver from a different angle: low-supplier shortages reach a final determination in a median of ~260 days, versus years for the rest.

LIMITATIONS

A risk signal to support your own judgment, not a certainty. Some categories rest on small sample sizes. Only drugs currently on the FDA shortage list are indexed — this is not a "will X go into shortage" predictor. Not a substitute for official FDA guidance.
